Make a tape. Press play. Get to work.

Blank Tape turns your own music into a cassette for focus. Fill side A and side B from your Apple Music library or your own files. One side is one work session: no skipping, only winding. When the side ends, the tape stops.

Free, no ads, no account, collects no data. For iPhone and iPad.
