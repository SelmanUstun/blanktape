Make a tape. Press PLAY. Hear it to the end.

Blank Tape turns your own music into a cassette. Fill side A and side B with songs or playlists from your Apple Music library, or with your own files. No skipping, only winding. When a side ends, flip it. There are skill tapes too, for recording 20 hours of practice on one skill.

Free to download, with an optional one-time Pro purchase. No ads, no account, collects no data. For iPhone and iPad.
