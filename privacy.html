Privacy Policy

Effective 18 November 2026 · Blank Tape for iPhone and iPad

Short version: Blank Tape collects no data. There is no account, no server, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything the app keeps stays on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. Blank Tape has no account, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking code. The app makes no network requests of its own. We, the developer, do not receive your tapes, your music, your listening habits or anything else from the app.

What stays on your device

  • Your tapes: tape names, cover colours, track lists and the position on each tape.
  • Your settings: for example key sounds, tape hiss and auto-reverse.
  • A short listening log: when you played, which tape and side, and for how long. It is used only inside the app, never leaves your device, and you can delete it at any time in Settings → Delete listening history.
  • Backup copies of your tape list inside the app's own storage, so your shelf can be restored if something goes wrong.

This data is stored in the app's private storage on your device. Like other app data, it can be included in your own iPhone or iPad backup (iCloud Backup or a computer backup), which Apple and you control. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Your music

  • Music library: if you allow access to Media & Apple Music, Blank Tape reads your library only to let you pick songs and to play them. Your library is not uploaded anywhere.
  • Apple Music: songs from Apple Music, and 30-second previews, are played through Apple's MusicKit. Apple handles those requests and the playback under Apple's Privacy Policy. Blank Tape does not see your Apple ID or your subscription details; it only asks iOS whether playback is allowed.
  • Imported files: audio files you import from the Files app are copied into the app's own storage on your device. They are never uploaded.

Permissions

  • Media & Apple Music: to list and play your songs. You can turn it off any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Media & Apple Music.
  • Notifications (optional): a local notification when a side ends. It is created on your device; no notification server is involved.
  • Live Activities, Shortcuts and Focus filters run on your device. If you use Siri, Apple processes your request under its own privacy policy.

Crash reports

If you chose to share analytics with app developers in iOS settings (Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements), Apple may send us anonymous crash reports. We receive them only through Apple and cannot identify you from them. You can turn this off at any time in the same place.

Children

Blank Tape collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date. If the app ever starts collecting data, this page and the App Store privacy label will say so before that version is released.
