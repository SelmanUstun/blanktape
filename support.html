Support

Blank Tape for iPhone and iPad · iOS 17 or later

Questions, bugs or ideas? Write to support@selmanustun.com. Please include your device, iOS version and what happened. We usually reply within a few days.

What Blank Tape needs

Blank Tape plays your own music: songs in your Apple Music library (an Apple Music subscription is needed for streamed songs), music you bought and downloaded to the device, or audio files you import from the Files app. Spotify and YouTube Music are not supported, because those services do not let other apps play their songs.

Frequently asked questions

Why can't I skip a song?

That is the idea. You choose the songs while making the tape, then press PLAY and listen to the side to the end. Like a real cassette, you can rewind and fast-forward, but there is no "next song" button.

How long can a side be?

C50 sides hold 25 minutes, C60 sides 30 minutes and C90 sides 45 minutes. A side may run up to 5 minutes over (the striped "slack" zone on the gauge). A song that would go past that does not fit.

A song is greyed out or says "download in Music".

The song is only in your iCloud Music Library and cannot be streamed. Open the Music app, download it, and it will be available in Blank Tape.

Blank Tape can't see my music.

Open Settings → Privacy & Security → Media & Apple Music and turn on Blank Tape. You can also tap Add song, then Import audio files, to use audio files from the Files app.

An Apple Music song stops or says "No signal".

Streamed Apple Music songs need an internet connection and an active subscription. Songs downloaded to your device and imported files play offline.

What happens when a side ends?

The tape stops, like a real cassette. Take a break, or flip the tape and play side B. If you prefer, turn on Auto-reverse in Settings: the tape turns over once and plays the other side.

Can I control it from the Lock Screen?

Yes. A Live Activity shows the tape on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, and the usual music controls and headphone buttons work. You can also use Shortcuts and a Focus filter that puts a chosen tape in the deck.

My shelf looks empty after an update or restart.

If your device was locked when the app started, Blank Tape waits until you unlock it and does not change anything. If a backup has more tapes than your shelf, the app offers to restore them. If something still looks wrong, write to us before deleting the app.

Can I put a playlist on a tape?

Yes. While making a tape, tap Playlists and choose one from your library. Songs that fit go on the sides; the app tells you how many were left out or can't be played.

Why is there no tape hiss?

Tape hiss starts switched off. You can turn it up in Settings.

What is a skill tape?

A tape for practice. You record fixed sessions of 15 to 60 minutes, and 20 hours earns the MASTER stamp. If you leave the app before a session ends, the tape tangles and those minutes do not count. Your voice is never recorded; only the time is counted.

What is free and what is Pro?

Free: 3 music tapes of any length on the shelf and one skill tape up to MASTER. Deleting a music tape frees its place. Blank Tape Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. It adds unlimited music tapes, unlimited skill tapes, recording past 20 hours, and tape lock for music tapes (needs a device passcode).

I installed Blank Tape before version 1.2. Am I limited to 3 tapes?

No. If you first installed Blank Tape before version 1.2, your music tapes stay unlimited. Tapes you already made always stay yours.

I can't see Pro.

Pro is not sold in the European Union at the moment. Where Pro is not sold, music tapes have no limit.

How do I restore Pro?

Open Blank Tape Pro and tap Restore Purchases, using the same Apple Account you bought it with. For refunds, use reportaproblem.apple.com; Apple handles all payments.

Does it cost anything?

Blank Tape is free to download, with no ads and no account. Pro is optional. The app collects no data; see the privacy policy.
